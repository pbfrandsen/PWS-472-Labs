Site frequency spectrum

How many sites have a variant carried by k sequences

A site with k carriers differs in k × (n − k) of the n(n − 1)/2 pairs, so it adds most to π when k is near n/2. Every site adds the same 1/a₁ to S/a₁.

Things to try

  1. Hold S fixed and drag the frequency slider from left to right. S/a₁ never moves, but π does, and D follows π.
  2. Press “Mostly rare” with S = 3, then raise S to 30. The pattern is the same, but with more sites D moves well past −2.
  3. Press “Neutral balance” and change n. a₁ grows slowly, so each extra sequence changes S/a₁ less than the one before.
  4. Drag the invariant-sites slider. The alignment gets longer, but π, S and D stay put: invariant sites never differ between sequences. Per-site diversity (π divided by length, which some software reports) does shrink, but D is unchanged.
